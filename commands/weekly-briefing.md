description: Weekly finance briefing — the one priority, what moved in cash, receivables and payables, and what can wait
argument-hint: "[company, optional]"
---

Write a weekly finance briefing for: $ARGUMENTS (default: the confirmed company, last 7 days vs the 7 days before). Load the review profile first.

Pull, in parallel where possible: balance sheet today and 7 days ago, A/R and A/P aging today, invoices issued and payments received this week and last, bills entered and paid this week and last.

Structure:
1. **The one priority** this week, with the number behind it.
2. **Cash:** opening, closing, the main movements.
3. **Receivables:** collected, new past due, top three overdue customers.
4. **Payables:** paid, due next 14 days, anything overdue.
5. **Ignore for now:** movements that look large but are expected (known context, timing).

Keep it under 250 words plus one table. Read-only. End with the **Data sources** line.
