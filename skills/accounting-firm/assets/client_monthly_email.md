# Client monthly email template

A short monthly update to one client. It's an **unsent draft** — the advisor edits and sends it. The client reads it on their phone; build for that.

---

**DRAFT — not sent. Review before sending.**

**Subject:** [Month] numbers — [headline, e.g. "best margin this year, two invoices to chase"]

Hi [client first name],

Your [month] books are [closed / ready for review]. The short version:

**The headline:** [One sentence with the result that matters most.]

- **Revenue:** [$] ([±%] vs [prior month / same month last year])
- **Net income:** [$] ([±%])
- **Cash at month end:** [$]

**What we need from you:**
- [Specific item, amount and date, e.g. "Receipt for the $2,140 Home Depot purchase on 12 Jul"]
- [Or: "Nothing this month."]

**Worth a conversation:** [One item, optional — e.g. "Two customers are over 60 days; want us to send reminders?"]

[Advisor name]

---

## Tone notes

- One client per email. Never mention, compare with or copy another client.
- Three numbers, at most. The full report is attached or linked.
- "What we need" is specific — amount, date, vendor. Vague asks don't get answered.
- If Gmail is connected, save as a draft; never send.
