## Tone notes

- Three topics, not seven. The client will remember one.
- Each topic has a number. A topic without a number is an opinion.
- Questions are open ("what's driving…"), not accusatory ("why did you…").
- Nothing from another client appears here — not even as a comparison, unless anonymized as "similar businesses in our book (n=…)".
