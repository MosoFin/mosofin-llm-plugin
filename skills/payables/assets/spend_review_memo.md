# Spend review memo template

A short memo on where the money is going and what moved, for an owner or management team. It leads with the category that changes a decision.

---

**Subject:** [Period] spend review — [headline, e.g. "contractor costs up 31%, everything else flat"]

**Company:** [display_name] · **Period:** [dates] · **Comparison:** [trailing 3-month average / prior YTD] · **Basis:** [Accrual / Cash]

**The headline:** [One sentence with the total change and the category behind it.]

**Categories that moved:**

| Category | Current | Comparison | Change | % | Driver | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Category] | [$] | [$] | [$] | [%] | [Driver from the records] | Observed / Possible / Unknown |

**Steady:** [One line naming the large categories that didn't move, so the reader knows they were checked.]

**Questions:**
1. [Specific question for the owner, tied to a category and amount]

**Data sources:** [datasource] · [tools] · fetched [fetched_at] (live / fixture)

---

## Tone notes

- Three categories that moved, at most. If more moved, the comparison period is probably unusual; say so.
- A planned increase the profile records (a new hire, a marketing push) is context, not a problem; colour it neutral.
- Recommendations to cut are questions until the owner answers them.
