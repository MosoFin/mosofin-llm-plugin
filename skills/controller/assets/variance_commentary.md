## Tone notes

- Lead with the variance that changes a decision. A $200 office supplies overrun is not the headline.
- F/U is by account type, not sign: revenue over budget is favourable.
- If the budget itself looks wrong (a month with zero budget for a recurring cost), say so rather than reporting a misleading variance.
