# Owner email template

The covering email for the owner pack or board deck. The owner reads it on a phone in 30 seconds, so it carries the headline, three numbers and one ask. It is always delivered as a draft for the user to send.

---

**Subject:** [Month/Quarter] financials — [headline, e.g. "revenue +8%, cash down $22k on late payments"]

Hi [Owner name],

Quick summary of [period] ahead of [meeting]:

**The headline:** [One sentence with direction, size and cause.]

**The numbers:**
- [Metric] — [value and change, with context]
- [Metric] — [value and change, with context]
- [Metric] — [value and change, with context]

**The ask:** [One specific decision or action, or "No ask — for awareness ahead of [meeting]".]

Full pack attached / linked at [location]. Happy to walk through it.

[Sender name]

---

## Tone notes

- Three bullets, each with a number.
- If the email mentions any forward-looking figure, it says "scenario" and the key assumption.
- Save as a draft through an email connector only if available and asked. Never send.
