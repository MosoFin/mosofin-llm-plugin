# Board / investor update outline

Slide-by-slide structure for a quarterly board or investor deck. The deck is a talking aid: one message per slide, a chart where numbers compare, bullets only.

---

**Slide 1 — Title.** [Company] · Q[N] [YYYY] update · [date]

**Slide 2 — The quarter in one line.** Headline sentence plus four metric cards: revenue, gross margin %, net income, ending cash — each with change vs prior quarter and vs last year.

**Slide 3 — Revenue and margin trend.** Monthly revenue bars with gross margin % line for the last 4–6 quarters. Callout: the biggest change and its driver.

**Slide 4 — KPI scorecard.** The committed KPIs vs target, status coloured. Callout on any KPI off target with its driver.

**Slide 5 — Cash and runway.** Month-end cash line with the minimum cash line; operating burn vs financing. Runway at current burn.

**Slide 6 — Customers.** Concentration (top-1, top-5 share) and changes in the top customers. Only if material.

**Slide 7 — What we tried.** Initiatives the user names, with what the numbers show. Include what didn't work.

**Slide 8 — Outlook (scenario).** Only if the user supplies assumptions. Labelled **Scenario — not a forecast**, assumptions listed on the slide.

**Slide 9 — Asks.** Specific decisions or help needed from the board, each with the number behind it. Or "No asks this quarter".

**Appendix.** Quarterly P&L summary, balance sheet summary, cash flow summary, definitions. Data sources slide: datasource, tools, `fetched_at`, live or fixture.

---

## Build notes

- Use the `pptx` skill after reading its SKILL.md.
- Combined figures across several companies are labelled "illustrative combined total — not a consolidation".
- No long text on slides; speaker notes carry the detail.
